# Phase 7 — Reporting and Analytics

Phase 7 turns NimbusBill operational data into customer, transaction, billing,
invoice, collection, and exception insights.

## Initial slice completed

- Cross-domain reporting API with date-range and customer filters.
- Customer, transaction, invoice, overdue, revenue, and collection KPIs.
- Transaction product and invoice status breakdowns.
- Reports workspace in the portal with CSV export.

## Remaining Phase 7 roadmap

- Time-series revenue and transaction-volume charts.
- Billing-account and product profitability reports.
- Reconciliation, rejection, and delivery-failure exception reports.
- Scheduled report generation and email delivery.
- Role-aware saved report definitions and dashboard widgets.
